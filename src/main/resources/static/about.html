About OILTRACE

An AI-powered maritime surveillance platform developed for Smart India Hackathon 2026. OILTRACE fuses SAR satellite imagery with AIS vessel transponder data to detect illegal oil discharges and attribute them to responsible vessels in near real-time.

MISSION

Marine oil pollution is one of the most persistent threats to ocean ecosystems. Detecting spills early and attributing responsibility is critical for enforcement, remediation, and deterrence. Traditional monitoring relies on costly aerial surveillance with limited coverage and delayed response.

OILTRACE leverages Sentinel-1 SAR satellite data — which can image through cloud cover at any time of day — combined with global AIS vessel tracking to provide continuous, automated monitoring of Exclusive Economic Zones. Deep learning segmentation models identify spill regions with >94% accuracy, while probabilistic trajectory analysis scores candidate vessels.

TECHNICAL PIPELINE
01
SAR Ingestion
GRD/SLC product preprocessing — radiometric calibration, speckle filtering (Lee 5×5), terrain correction.
02
Spill Segmentation
U-Net with ResNet-50 encoder trained on annotated Sentinel-1 scenes. Outputs per-pixel spill probability mask.
03
Spill Characterisation
Connected-component labelling, area estimation (geographic projection), centroid coordinates, severity grading.
04
AIS Retrieval
Query AIS historical archive for vessels within 50 nm of spill centroid in ±6h acquisition window.
05
Attribution Scoring
Trajectory interpolation, spatial proximity, temporal correlation, and vessel-class oil-carriage probability combined into composite score.
